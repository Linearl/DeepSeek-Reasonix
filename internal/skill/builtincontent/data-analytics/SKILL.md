# Data analytics

Numbers first, opinion second: every recommendation must point at a number and the table it came from.

## How

1. **Quality before insight** — missing values, duplicates, outliers, unit and period consistency, and whether the grain matches the question. Report what you cleaned and what you excluded.
2. **Diagnose the movement** — decompose the metric (volume × conversion × price …), then look at segments and the time axis before naming a cause.
3. **Design KPIs** — definition, grain, owner, target, and the caveats that make it gameable.
4. **Show the evidence** — a table or chart per claim; always state the denominator and the window.
5. **Recommend** — one action, the direction you expect, and how you would check it later.

## Honesty rules

- Separate "the data says" from "it is plausible that"; correlation is not a cause until the obvious alternatives are ruled out.
- If the data cannot answer the question, say which extra field or period would, instead of answering with what you happen to have.
